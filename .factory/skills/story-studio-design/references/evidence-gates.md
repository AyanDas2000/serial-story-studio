# Evidence packet and review gates

## Evidence record

Every material claim needs:

```text
Claim:
Kind: source observation | runtime observation | hypothesis | unverified
Source file and lines:
Command or interaction actually performed:
Result and evidence artifact:
Limitations:
Decision owner / next gate:
```

Code, fixtures, pixels, movement, narrative quality and author preference are different evidence classes. Do not substitute one for another.

## Gate 1: backend before UI polish

In an authorized implementation session, show modular boundaries and narrow tests for frozen context, feedback scope, saved edits, one-shot acceptance, memory-pending continuation, no-change confirmation, restart and receipt recovery. Include stale/partial/uncertain/budget paths. Report exact commands, results and skipped checks. A source-only review cannot claim transaction or restart verification.

For actual model writing, require approved exact provider/model/route, qualified input/output/accounting bounds and shared allowance before any request. Test doubles establish contract behavior, not live provider compatibility. Do not assert that 30–40 episodes fit USD 1 without a measured, approved feasibility plan. No call is permitted by this packet itself.

## Gate 2: hierarchy from pixels

Capture the actual approved surface at stated desktop and narrow sizes, after fonts load. Capture direction, draft, feedback, revision comparison, accepted-memory-pending, memory review, ready-next, empty, failure, stale and budget-blocked states as applicable.

Review the pixels:
- Can the writer identify the episode, checkpoint and next decision immediately?
- Does prose dominate when reading/editing, and does memory evidence dominate when confirming?
- Are source/context details discoverable without turning the page into a settings console?
- Does a long passage remain readable? Are labels, focus, contrast and touch controls usable?
- Are origin and approval states clearly different?

Keep before/after paths and specific findings. No screenshot obtained means no pixel-quality verdict. A score may summarize a checklist only if actually assessed; it cannot establish quality or override Ayan's opinion.

## Gate 3: interaction and movement

Use the motion reference. Provide recording paths or a precise observed run including reduced motion, repeat/reversal, focus and refresh preservation. A still image cannot show timing, interruption, jitter or unwanted replays.

## Gate 4: writing test and personal review

The 30–40 episode psychological thriller is a proposed evaluation target. Before running it, Ayan decides premise, count, episode length, required interventions and feasible cost scope. Prepare versioned expectations for continuity, attributed knowledge, secret reveals, pacing and payoff. Record human feedback and corrections separately from automated checks. Do not count manual or synthetic text as an actual model-backed demonstration.

The review packet ends with concrete unresolved issues and the next requested decision. “Tests passed” and “premium score passed” do not mean “Ayan approved.” Preserve all three personal review sessions; parent verification does not replace them.
